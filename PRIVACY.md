# Privacy

This page is about the Google application "Dashboard perso". Its author uses it alone, with a single Google account of their own; it is offered to no one else.

- Data: what the author authorizes on that account, starting with Google Tasks.
- Storage: on the author's own machines, a Mac and a home server. Access tokens stay in the macOS Keychain, and on the server in encrypted or owner-only files.
- Voice assistants: with the local model, nothing leaves these machines. With Claude Haiku, the text needed for a request goes to Anthropic to answer it.
- No sale, no advertising, no other sharing.
- Removing access: https://myaccount.google.com/permissions
- Contact: an issue on this repository.
